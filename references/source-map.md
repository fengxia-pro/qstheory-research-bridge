# Qstheory Source Map

Use this file when choosing sources from Qstheory/Qiushi.

## Primary URLs

- Homepage: https://www.qstheory.cn/
- 科教 channel: https://www.qstheory.cn/science/
- English mirror when useful for translation context: https://en.qstheory.cn/

## High-Signal Sections

Prioritize these homepage or issue sections when present:

- 求是专区
- 当前期目录 and individual 《求是》 issue pages
- 本刊编辑部文章
- 社论评论
- 学习问答
- 深度调研
- 求是专访
- 专家学者
- A智荟 / 青年悦读汇 when interpreting AI, law, economy, or public-understanding themes
- 科教 channel for education, science, technology, talent, AI, and innovation ecosystem signals

## Article-Type Priority

1. 习近平重要文章 and central-level speeches published in 《求是》.
2. 《求是》杂志编辑部文章, 评论员文章, 社论评论, 学习问答.
3. Deep research, interviews, expert-scholar essays.
4. Qstheory reposts from People's Daily, Xinhua, Guangming Daily, Economic Daily, Science and Technology Daily, and ministry sources.
5. News briefs only when they reveal a concrete policy release, regulation, program, or funding mechanism.
